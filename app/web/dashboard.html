About this dashboard

Municipal demonstration on fictitious data. Figures are computed live from the current state:

  • Reports: timestamped receipts within the period (dateless scenarios excluded from windows).
  • Incidents: current reconstruction (one multi-problem report may count several times).
  • Teams: simulated teams available / total.
  • Budget: 120 fictitious units (neither francs, nor euros, nor dollars).
  • Statuses: exclusive and defined (S5 escalation, planned, unassigned, to review).

Scientific protocol not frozen — no proof of impact.
