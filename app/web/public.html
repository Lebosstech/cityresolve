    About CityResolve

    CityResolve turns disorganized urban observations into distinct, geolocated, prioritized incidents, then into feasible intervention proposals.

    1. Report: description, photo, position on the demo grid.
    2. Analyse: multiple-problem detection, grouping of similar reports, unknowns kept.
    3. Plan: the municipal area proposes interventions under constraints (teams, deadlines, budget).
